# T06: About 2,000-word spec with three questions for applicants

Hello, I can build your lead routing system using n8n and PostgreSQL. My goal is to automate intake from your four sources, validate and deduplicate records, enrich them, apply your routing rules, mirror data to Google Sheets, and notify reps in Slack while keeping everything error-resistant and fully documented. My plan starts with setting up the PostgreSQL schema and intake workflows, followed by validation and duplicate merging, then enrichment, routing rules, and Slack alerts. I did nearly this for a lead intake automation with n8n: lead entry dropped from about 2 hours a week to under 10 minutes. For PostgreSQL, I propose a schema featuring a leads table, a touchpoints table, and an assignments table. Would you like to review the proposed table schema before our kickoff?

**How would you keep the round-robin fair and correct when two leads arrive in the same second?**
I would store the round-robin pointer in PostgreSQL and use atomic updates or database locks to ensure fair and accurate assignments during concurrent arrivals.

**How would you parse partner referral emails that don't follow a fixed format, and what would go to the review queue?**
I would route unparseable partner emails into a dedicated review queue table so no data is dropped while refining the extraction rules.

**Which part of this project do you see as the biggest risk, and how would you reduce it in the first milestone?**
Unstructured partner referral emails are the biggest risk, and I would reduce it in the first milestone by implementing the review queue table for any failed extractions.
